desire - deception

why must one choose between doing what they want and choosing what is 'right'. consequences breathe down my neck, strangle my every decision. if only you knew how they crawl up my legs, tethering me to the ground. flightless creature bound by its tongue. nest escapable if you are able to survive helpless.

and no matter how far, i shall let my thoughts be expressed.
