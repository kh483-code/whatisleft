frosted glass

i am not myself, nor am i anyone else. the me who exists is a spitting image of what those who know me want me to be. the i ive known to be myself has long died, a quiet peaceful death. the me who people talk to is the real me, full of emotion yet empty at the same time. a frosted glass stands between us, both me and not me, and i choose to run my finger along it. of course, this is exactly what i would do in this situation so the i i know is real and alive. yet i choose to perceive myself as false, for reasons unknown.

and no matter how far, i shall let my thoughts be expressed.
