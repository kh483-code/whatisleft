ripples

To say you are changing is to begin the process of erasing who you once were.
Minimal changes add up, converging into a new identity, a shell of who you once were.
It acts as an armor, protecting the innocence and naivety of who you once were.
Who you once were is nothing but a ripple in a lake. Outer rings build up and around. The you who stands before yourself rewrites who you once were.

and no matter how far, i shall let my thoughts be expressed.
