to those unfortunate enough to read this

Life isn't fair. To those who try and to those who do not. To the ones who follow every rule and the ones who break them all. Every path you take is both the best and worst thing you will ever do, no way of knowing another. My life is what it has become because I did what I did. To choose another road is to forego myself.

thoughts (unheard)

I've decided to start writing. Call it poetry but that would be a lie. This is all merely a conjunction of words in my head that don't necessarily connect. Strung together, like all in the world is.

my carcass, bare and exposed.

What is to become of me? Will I become a better person by doing this or will I be consumed by my mind? Will this bring me salvation or will I wither away? This is all just another path I take in order to become myself.

and no matter how far, i shall let my thoughts be expressed.
